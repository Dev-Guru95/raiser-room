Navy      #101F3F   primary — grounds, headers, body type
Gold      #C9A227   accent — the mark, rules, emphasis
Cream     #F4EFE4   light ground
Burgundy  #6E1F33   supporting — secondary surfaces, alerts, charts

Display and wordmark: Cinzel Bold.

Gold is an accent and never body text — it fails contrast at small sizes
on both navy and cream. For body text use cream on navy, or navy on cream.
